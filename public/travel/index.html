How it works

  1. 1
    Type or say it in English

    Use the 🎙 button to just speak. No Korean needed.

  2. 2
    coverfo picks the Korean app

    It works out what you want and opens the official app or site — with the place, date or number of people filled in where possible.

  3. 3
    Finish in that app

    You book and pay directly in the official app or site. coverfo never takes your payment.

📲
Keep it on your home screen. In Chrome: ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone Safari: Share □↑ → Add to Home Screen. Then coverfo opens like an app, any time you need it.

Good to know

  • coverfo only opens the official apps and websites — it doesn't book, reserve or take payment for you.
  • Some Korean apps may ask you to install the app or sign up the first time you use them.
  • Prices, rooms and availability come from each app or site, not from coverfo.
  • On the coverfo home screen, 🌐 통역톡 (Translate Talk) helps you chat with people in Korean and other languages.
